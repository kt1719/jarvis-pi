Privacy Policy

Jarvis Pi is a private, single-user application run on hardware owned by its sole user.

  • It accesses Google services (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) only for the account of its owner, who is the only user.
  • Data is processed locally on the owner's own device and is not sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • No data is retained beyond what is needed to perform the requested action.
  • Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account > Security > Third-party access.

Contact: the application owner, via the developer contact email listed on the Google consent screen.
